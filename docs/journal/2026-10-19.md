## In my own words

### Token

A **token** is a small piece of text the model reads and generates—not always a whole word. The API counts tokens for billing and limits; our server reports them under `usage` in chat responses. Shorter prompts leave more room for the answer inside the context window.

### Context

**Context** is everything the model can attend to in one request: system message, chat history, retrieved docs (later), and the reply being generated. With `-c 4096`, prompt + completion must fit in roughly 4096 tokens total or we risk truncation or errors.
